## The one thing
Market commentary takes the most effort and returns the least. On LinkedIn it is 44% of posts but 26% of engagement: under-delivers, at roughly four-fifths of a typical post. Posts that name a partner do about twice as well as those that don't (about 2.0× versus 0.8× on LinkedIn). The same gap appears on X (2.0× versus 1.0×) and Telegram (1.4× versus 0.8×). Fewer generic commentary posts and more named partners and guests is the clearest lever.
