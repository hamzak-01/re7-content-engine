## The one thing

The Weekly-style market commentary absorbs the most posting effort and returns the least. On LinkedIn it is 45% of posts but 26% of engagement (return on effort 0.58). On X it is 19% of posts and 6% of engagement (0.30), and on Telegram it scores 0.83. Several of the worst posts on each channel are Weekly announcements, with LinkedIn lows at 0.14. The content may be sound, but announcing it as a link is not working. We should cut its frequency and repackage it.

## Do more of

**Labs product posts on X.** They are 21% of posts and 55% of engagement (2.61), with the best resonance of any theme at 15.6 likes per 1,000 views. On Telegram the same theme returns 1.35.

**Media appearances.** They return 1.70 on LinkedIn and average 3.97 on X. Video on X averages 3.18 (n=4).

**Naming a partner.** Posts that name one average 2.01 against 0.85 on LinkedIn, and 1.98 against 1.03 on X.

## Do less of (or do differently)

Chart posts return 0.48 on LinkedIn and 0.34 on X. Fold the chart into a commentary or research post rather than posting it alone.

Speaking events on X are 16% of posts for 6% of engagement (0.38), yet average 1.61 on LinkedIn. Keep them mainly on LinkedIn.

Telegram "pinned a photo" posts score 0.0 and should stop.

## Where the same content travels best

X wins most of the time. The Binance hack Weekly scored 2.36 on X against 0.62 on LinkedIn. The DeFi risk framework did well on both X (3.09) and LinkedIn (2.97). Telegram is lowest for every shared piece (0.11–0.59) and has fallen each week from 0.52 to 0.11. Treat Telegram as the Labs product channel and send only a one-line link to the Weekly there. On X, lead with the single sharpest point from each piece.
