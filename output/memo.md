## The one thing
Market commentary is where the effort goes and where the return is weakest. On LinkedIn it is 44% of posts but 25% of engagement: under-delivers. On X it is 17% of posts but 5% of engagement: under-delivers. Posts built around a named person or partner do far better: on LinkedIn, posts naming a partner average about 1.9× a typical post against roughly 0.9× without one. The implication is to shift weight from anonymous commentary towards posts anchored on people and partners.

## Do more of
Labs product posts on X: 19% of posts and 53% of engagement, outperforms. They average about 2.1× a typical post, and the best X post of the period (about 10×) was a lending-yield post. Telegram agrees: 42% of posts and 56% of engagement, outperforms.

Media appearances on LinkedIn (15% of posts, 26% of engagement, outperforms) and on X (about 3.2× a typical post). Video on X averages about 2.5×, though on only five posts.

## Do less of (or do differently)
LinkedIn market commentary and chart posts: commentary is 44% of posts for 25% of engagement; charts are 14% of posts for 7%. Fold the chart into a named-person or partner post instead of running it alone.

X speaking-event posts (16% of posts, 6% of engagement), chart posts (11% and 4%) and commentary all under-deliver. Cut the frequency, and where the event features a named speaker, post it as a short video or image with that person tagged.

## Where the same content travels best
The Weekly does best on X or LinkedIn only when it has a distinctive angle. The AI risk-framework piece reached about 2.9× on LinkedIn and 2.8× on X but only about 0.6× on Telegram. The Binance piece did about 2.5× on X and 0.6× on LinkedIn. Telegram rated every one of the last five Weeklies at or below about 0.6×, so it is not where research lands. Lead on X with the single sharpest claim; on LinkedIn, with the named framework and author.

## Who moves the needle
Joseph Chalom (Sharplink) appears in six posts averaging about 4.1× a typical post; Sharplink as a partner averages about 3.7× across seven. Zodia Custody (about 3.8×), BCB Group (about 3.3×), USDai (about 6.1×, two posts) and Peter Smith (about 4.1×, three posts) follow. Tactic: book guests from this group, and ask partners to repost when they appear.

2. Add one Labs product post on X with an image or video; this theme earns about 2.8 times its share of attention.

Telegram's Friday posts also average about 1.8× against roughly 0.8× on Mondays, while Monday is where LinkedIn posts most often (42 of 85).
